**🎤 2-Minute Pitch: Sentinel-RTI — Automated Citizen Advocacy Agent**

**Problem**
In India, millions of citizens face daily civic issues—potholes, garbage, broken streetlights—but very few take formal action.
Why? Because filing an RTI or complaint is complex, confusing, and often ignored.

People don’t know the correct department, struggle with legal language, and even when they complain, lack of proper evidence and follow-up leads to inaction.

---

**Solution**
We built **Sentinel-RTI**, an AI-powered civic advocacy platform that transforms any citizen into an effective problem-solver.

Users can simply type their issue in plain Hindi or English—or upload an image.
Our system then converts it into a **legally structured, evidence-backed complaint** ready for submission.

With our **AI Evidence Enhancer**, the platform:

* Detects issues from images (like potholes or garbage)
* Adds timestamp and geo-location
* Generates a severity-based, formal description

This turns a simple complaint into a **strong, data-backed case that authorities cannot easily ignore**.

---

**Approach**
Our system is built on the MERN stack with AI at its core:

* **React** for a simple, accessible user interface
* **Node + Express** for backend automation and workflows
* **MongoDB** to track the full lifecycle of complaints

To ensure legal accuracy, we integrate **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**.
This allows our AI to fetch real provisions from the RTI Act and government guidelines before generating the complaint.

So every submission is not just well-written—but **legally grounded, precise, and far more likely to be accepted**.

We also implement an **automation engine** that:

* Routes complaints to the correct authority
* Tracks deadlines
* Sends intelligent follow-ups
* Automatically generates appeals if ignored

And to stay compliant with government systems, we use a **human-in-the-loop model** for OTP and CAPTCHA verification.

---

**Vision**
Sentinel-RTI is not just a complaint tool—it’s an **AI-powered accountability system**.

We are turning scattered citizen complaints into **structured, evidence-backed, legally accurate, and persistent actions**.

Because real change doesn’t happen when complaints are made—
It happens when they are **proven, tracked, and enforced**.
